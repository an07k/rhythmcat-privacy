03

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Identifying your device so your progress and leaderboard entry persist across sessions
  • Saving and syncing your game progress
  • Displaying leaderboards with nicknames and scores
  • Serving ads and measuring ad performance (see Advertising below)
  • Diagnosing bugs, crashes, and performance issues
  • Improving gameplay balance and difficulty tuning based on aggregate usage patterns
  • Complying with legal obligations where required

We do not sell your data to marketing companies. Our only advertising partner is Google AdMob, used to display in-app ad s as described below.

  • Advertising ID / App Set ID
  • IP address (used to estimate your general location)
  • Device and ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic and crash information

You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time from your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google Set tings → Ads). For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Data Security

We rely on Firebase and Google AdMob's platform-standard security practices to protect your data, including:

  • Data transmission encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to leaderboard data restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security reviews of our systems

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

07

Data Retention

We retain your leaderboard entry (nickname and score) for as long as it remains linked to your active device/sign-in iden tifier. If you request deletion, we will permanently erase your personal information within 30 days, except wh ere we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Anonymized, aggregate analytics data (which cannot identify you) may be retained by Google per its own retention policies .

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the leaderboard data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your leaderboard entry and identifier
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, such as analytics
