01

Overview

Rhythm Cat is a mobile rhythm game for Android where players tap and interact in sync with music. To provide features like account saving, leaderboards, and progress tracking, we collect a limited set of personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rhythm Cat mobile application and describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the game. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Rhythm Cat:

👤
Account Info
Username, email address, encrypted password
🎮
Game Data
Scores, progress, completed levels, achievements
📱
Device Info
Device model, OS version, language settings
📊
Usage Data
Session durations, feature interactions, crash reports

We do not collect payment information directly — any in-app purchases are processed by the Google Play Store under Google's privacy policy.

03

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your Rhythm Cat account
  • Saving and syncing your game progress across sessions
  • Displaying leaderboards and personal high scores
  • Diagnosing bugs, crashes, and performance issues
  • Sending important service notifications (e.g. account security, major updates)
  • Improving gameplay balance and difficulty tuning based on aggregate usage patterns
  • Complying with legal obligations where required

We do not use your data for targeted advertising or sell it to marketing companies.

04

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the game (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics). These providers are contractually bound to keep your data confidential and use it only for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal requirements: If required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With your consent: For any other purpose, only with your explicit permission.

05

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Passwords stored using strong one-way hashing (bcrypt)
  • Data transmission encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to user data restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security reviews of our systems

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, please contact us immediately.

06

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will permanently erase your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Anonymized, aggregate analytics data (which cannot identify you) may be retained indefinitely to improve the game.

07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your account and personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, such as analytics

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed below. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Children's Privacy

Rhythm Cat is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

For users between 13 and 18, we encourage parental involvement and oversight of their account usage.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the game evolves — for example, when iOS support is introduced or new features are added. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or via the email address associated with your account.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Rhythm Cat after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
